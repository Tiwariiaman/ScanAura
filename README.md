# 🚀 ScanAura

> Digital Business Profile & QR Menu Platform for Local Businesses

ScanAura is a SaaS platform that helps businesses create a professional digital presence. Businesses can register, manage products/services, generate QR codes, and share their catalog with customers instantly.

---

## ✨ Features

### Business
- Business Registration
- Secure Login (JWT)
- Business Profile Management
- Subscription Plans

### Catalog
- Categories
- Products / Services
- Pricing
- Images
- Availability

### QR
- Dynamic QR Generation
- Public Business Landing Page

### Customer
- View Catalog
- Contact Business
- UPI Payment
- Social Media Links

### Admin
- User Management
- Business Approval
- Subscription Management
- Dashboard & Analytics
